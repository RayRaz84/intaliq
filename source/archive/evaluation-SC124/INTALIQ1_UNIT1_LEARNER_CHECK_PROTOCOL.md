# Intaliq 1 — Unit 1 learner / panel check: protocol and instrument, v2 (not yet run)

*Editor (Text role) · opened 2026-10-11 under SC-121 · **v2 2026-10-13 under SC-122**, which merged the instructional auditor's `INTALIQ1_UNIT1_LEARNING_CHECK_ADDENDUM.md` into this protocol and reconciled the closure rule (§9). The instrument for the small instructional verification that no build can do: the questions, the materials, the sheets, and what counts. It can be run by the owner, a panel educator or a teacher with three to six children (ages 8–13, decoding assumed, no Arabic vocabulary assumed), or by the expert panel in the owner's place (charter §8 as amended by SC-009) — the two kinds of sitting produce two different kinds of evidence, named in §9. **It has not been run; nothing below is a result.** Results, when they exist, are written by the observer into `INTALIQ1_UNIT1_LEARNING_EVIDENCE_RECORD.md`, never here. **The printable observer materials — brief, Check A minute by minute, Check B's observation points, every probe written out and licence-checked, the sheets and the sheet → record → revision route — are `INTALIQ1_UNIT1_OBSERVATION_KIT.md` (SC-123); this protocol says what is run and what counts, the kit says how.** Current material: Studio Version 47.*

## 0 · What this check can and cannot establish

Check A (§1–§4) asks three questions: cold Reading 1 comprehension, survey-to-N2 transfer, independent N3 completion. It does not establish delayed retention, listening comprehension, speaking across the unit, Reading 2 comprehension or the authentic corner — those belong to Check B (§5), the delayed probe (§7), and to inputs not yet delivered (§6). Check A gives Phase 1 ten minutes of a 48-task pathway and allows stopping anywhere; a learner who reached task 20 received **restricted preparation**, and a difficulty after it is evidence about pacing or delivery, not a failure of the design after the promised preparation. Check A's running order goes from Reading 1 to C1 although C1's possession language is also reinforced by Reading 2; a sitting may restrict input on purpose, but **it is a diagnostic, never a trial of the complete five-phase unit**. The teacher reading the C1 questions once is planned rehearsal; any further prompting is extra assistance and is recorded as such (standard §5).

## 1 · Check A — the cold-reading diagnostic: three questions

| # | Question | What is observed | Descriptive indicator (not a pass mark — standard §7) |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | **Cold comprehension.** Can a learner who has done only Phase 1 (the picture spread and the digital vocabulary pathway) understand Reading 1 on a first, unassisted read? | after reading «قَائِمَةُ رَزَان» alone, the learner answers the four صَحٌّ أَمْ خَطَأٌ؟ items and the five مَنْ يَقُولُ؟ items on the page, without help, then **points to the line that shows it** for one item of each kind (meaning confirmation by low-output response; picture-only success is recorded as picture inference, not text comprehension); the observer notes where the reading stalled | ≥ 3 of 4 and ≥ 3 of 5 from the page alone, for most of the group — retained as a local descriptive indicator; it does not by itself establish learning or close any other function |
| B | **Survey-to-writing transfer.** Does the survey (C1, Phase 3) produce what the parallel story (N2, Phase 4) needs? | the group runs C1 from notebook p. 9 with the three printed questions; each learner then completes p. 5–6 **alone** from their own grid; the observer notes whether the learner understands the answers they marked, not only that the grid is full | ≥ 3 names on every grid; p. 5–6 with real names from the grid; صَدِيقِي / صَدِيقَتِي chosen correctly for the friend named; questions the learner could not ask without the teacher's voice recorded |
| C | **Independent notebook completion.** Can a learner finish p. 7 («قِصَّتِي») with only the page in front of them? | p. 7 alone: the four frames, the two banks, the printed models, the speech bank | all four lines with bank words in the right slots (a thing after عِنْدَهُ / عِنْدَهَا, a person after «لِي ___»), a drawing, no question asked of an adult; a copied past form is **not** awarded as independent production (G-input_first) |

Nothing else is measured in Check A. No scores are kept beyond the sheet; no child's name leaves the room (SC-048 item 7 applies to the sitting as to the platform).

## 3 · Running order for Check A (one sitting, about 40 minutes)

1. Before anything: verify decoding (each child reads one line of the picture spread aloud); summarise prior Arabic exposure without names (standard §6 — a child who decodes is not assumed to know no Arabic).
2. Phase 1 — 10 min: the picture spread, then the digital vocabulary pathway on the shared device (48 tasks); **record the task number reached** and whether preview speech was played. If the pathway was not finished, either finish it before the reading (and record the extra time) or label every later result **restricted preparation**.
3. Reading 1, alone — 8 min: silent read; صَحٌّ أَمْ خَطَأٌ؟ and مَنْ يَقُولُ؟ on the page; the pointing confirmation. Observer records answers, stall points, picture-only cases (**A**).
4. C1 — 7 min: notebook p. 9; the printed questions read aloud once by the teacher (planned), then learners ask each other. Observer records every further prompt (**B**).
5. N2 — 8 min: p. 5–6 alone from the grid (**B**).
6. N3 — 7 min: p. 7 alone (**C**).
7. The observer completes §4 before anyone leaves.

## 5 · Check B — performance after the actual unit sequence

Run inside ordinary teaching, at the pace the learners' processing sets; **never compressed into the Check A sitting**. Record the exposure actually given before each observation. One row per stage in the evidence record §C.

| Stage | Observe | Resource / support |
|---|---|---|
| Input and repeated processing | Can learners identify meaning across more than one occurrence? Where does decoding or meaning break down? | the phrase page and the digital pathways; record completion and whether preview speech was used |
| Both readings | Can learners follow who is speaking, the friendship problem and the pen's unexpected intervention? | canon and strips; separate textual evidence from picture inference |
| C1 → N2 | Do exchanged names become appropriate names in the parallel writing? Does the learner understand the answers they mark? | printed questions, models, grid and N2; planned rehearsal allowed |
| N1 / N3 | Are framed lines meaningful and referents coherent? Does the learner select things and people appropriately? | banks and models; past forms copied, not formed; no new paradigm demand |
| C3 / C4 | Can the learner make a short supported contribution and respond to a partner? | the completed page, familiar language, the teacher's frame cue appropriate to Captions |
| Functional texts | Can the learner locate a named person or useful information? | the roster; authentic-photo tasks only when the photographs exist |

No independent production is awarded for a copied past form. No free retell and no fully plotted original story is imposed on the Unit 1 Captions rung. Support-only forms are never assessed productively.

## 6 · Listening and the authentic corner

Until the 72 recordings are verified and bound (`INTALIQ1_UNIT1_AUDIO_INTAKE.md`), listening is recorded as **not delivered / not observed**; preview synthesis does not satisfy SC-028. When the clips exist: at least one phrase-to-picture and one sentence-or-speaker probe, sound before transcript, the one planned replay allowed and recorded, extra prompting recorded; clip accuracy inspected separately from learner comprehension. Until the three photographs exist (`INTALIQ1_UNIT1_AUTHENTIC_CORNER_INTAKE.md`), the corner is **not observed**; when they exist, first verify that the name or letter the task needs is legible from the photograph at page size — a photograph's presence is not evidence that its task works.

## 7 · The delayed probe and small transfer

At the next available lesson — 3–7 days later is guidance for this first local trial, not a pacing rule — a brief check **before** any review, the actual interval and intervening exposure recorded. One identity/friendship meaning and one possession meaning through familiar, authorized language in a different response order or a licensed recombination; a short exchange with a different partner, printed question and model support still allowed at this rung; a changed-name or changed-object caption or framed line from the licensed bank, the change small enough that new language cannot explain a failure; a listening meaning check with a verified clip once recordings exist. **Probes are written and licence-checked before the day** (`apparatus_licence_u1.json`; the canons); nothing is improvised in the room. A repeated verbatim response supports memory of that response; changed-context success supports bounded transfer; neither establishes general proficiency.

## 8 · What the result changes

Per function, the observer's record is read as **supported by the observation**, **revise and recheck**, or **insufficient evidence** (standard §7). A difficulty is investigated before it is blamed on vocabulary load: decoding, meaning, instructions, task design, technology, attention or unknown. Missing or ambiguous resources, incoherent referents or language outside the envelope trigger a materials correction; repeated difficulty after real preparation sends the input load, repeated processing or supports back for review; a full grid without understanding prompts a meaning check; a failure after restricted preparation is evidence about pacing or delivery, not a clean test of learning. A fail on A sends the preparation sequence back for redesign, never the stories; a fail on B the survey questions or the N2 frame; a fail on C the N3 models and banks. The frozen stories stand (SC-093) unless their own meaning or discourse is the identified problem and the owner reopens them. Every revision is recorded function → observation → explanation to test → source correction → expected improvement → recheck (template §E).
