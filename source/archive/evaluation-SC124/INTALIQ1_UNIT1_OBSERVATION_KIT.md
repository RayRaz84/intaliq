## 1 · Observer's brief (read before the sitting; keep it to one page)

You are recording what learners **do** with the printed and digital material, under what support, after how much preparation. You are not grading anyone and nothing you write leaves the room with a name on it (SC-048 item 7): use labels A–F for the children in a sitting, and keep any sheet that links a label to a child in your own hands only.

Four labels describe every observed response (standard §5): **within planned support** (only the resources the task prints or the protocol allows), **extra assistance** (you or a peer gave a cue, a translation, a reformulation or a rereading), **answer supplied** (someone effectively gave the answer), **not observed** (absent, not reached, asset missing). A printed bank, a strip picture or the one planned replay is *within planned support*. When a learner struggles, write the reason as best you can judge — decoding · meaning · instructions · task design · technology · attention · unknown — and do not default to "vocabulary".

You may clarify **instructions** in the children's language outside the Arabic task ("the circle is where the number goes"); you may not translate the Arabic of a task or point to the answer. If you do, label it *extra assistance* or *answer supplied* and carry on; a labelled intervention is good data, an unlabelled one is a false pass.

**Preparation is recorded, not assumed.** Before Reading 1, write the vocabulary-pathway task number reached (of 48). If it was not finished, either finish it (and record the time) or label every later result **RESTRICTED PREPARATION**.

## 7 · After the sitting — from sheet to record to revision

1. Transfer counts and labels to `INTALIQ1_UNIT1_LEARNING_EVIDENCE_RECORD.md` §B (administration), §C (one row per function or recurring problem) and §D (the ledger row for the Version observed); write *restricted preparation* where it applies. Labels A–F, never names.
2. For each essential function decide **supported / revise and recheck / insufficient evidence** (protocol §8; standard §7); the 3/4 and 3/5 counts are descriptive only.
3. For every *revise*: §E of the record — observed difficulty → explanation to test → the **authoritative source** to change (engine, Studio source, notebook block, teacher data) → expected learner behaviour afterwards → the Version that will carry the recheck. Frozen stories are not touched (SC-093) unless the owner reopens them.
4. A panel sitting is written into §D as **design review**; learner rows stay *not observed* (SC-122). A learner sitting fills *observed performance*; the delayed sitting fills *delayed* and *transfer*.
5. Public wording, if any, waits for the owner and is recorded with its register reference (standard §8).
