## C. Observation record

Use one row per observed function or recurring problem. Per-learner working notes, if needed, stay local; share aggregates without identities. Give denominators and missing observations explicitly. Do not place names or individual scores in this record.

| Function and task | Initial performance / meaning evidence | Planned support used | Extra assistance / answer supplied | Delayed or changed-context result | Count and denominator | Alternative explanation / missing evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Not observed | No result | No result | No result | No result | No result | Trial not run |

Also note plot comprehension, persistence, confusing rubrics, decoding stalls and device constraints. Keep preference statements separate from performance evidence.

## F. Administration integrity

- Confirm actual preparation before interpreting a cold-read result.
- Distinguish comprehension of Arabic from inference from an illustration.
- Avoid revealing the listening transcript before the initial response.
- Separate bank-supported writing from independently formed language.
- Record partner support and teacher interventions.
- Avoid repeated identical questions as the sole evidence of retention.
- Use authorized language and the current output rung for every probe.
- No fabricated results, learner identities, product grades or answer-key publication.
