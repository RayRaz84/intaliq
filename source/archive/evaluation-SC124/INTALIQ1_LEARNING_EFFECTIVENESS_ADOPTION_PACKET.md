## 1 · What the package asks the course to adopt

One standard and one habit. The standard says that technical verification, design review, observed learner performance, delayed performance and transfer are **five different kinds of evidence**, reported separately, each supporting only its own claim; that every unit carries an evidence plan beside its Gate A brief; that "not run" is a valid status and is never converted to "pass"; and that no software test, encounter count or panel role-play can produce an observed-learning label. The habit is the evidence record per unit, filled only by a named observer at a real sitting.

Everything else in the course is explicitly left standing: frozen canons, licences, the closed activity catalog, SC-048 (no scores, no keys, no accounts), SC-028, the panel route of SC-009. The standard creates no activity family, no test product, no learner-data practice.

## 2 · What the Text role verified in the package

The auditor's reading of the record was checked line by line against the instruments; every citation holds. Three points deserve the owner's eye.

- **The ambiguity is real.** SC-121 closes Unit 1 on "the learner check … run and recorded"; the protocol it names (`INTALIQ1_UNIT1_LEARNER_CHECK_PROTOCOL.md`) says in its first paragraph that the check may be run "by the expert panel in the owner's place (charter §8 as amended by SC-009)". Both are my own wording, written the same day; they do not agree on whether a panel sitting closes the unit. SC-009's own residual note already says what a panel cannot do: "the behavior of the scripted materials in untrained hands in live classrooms remains unobserved … a known-open risk, not a blocker."
- **The ten-minute preparation.** The protocol's running order gives Phase 1 ten minutes and allows stopping anywhere in the 48-task pathway. The auditor is right that a learner who reaches task 20 has been given restricted input; the protocol records the task number but does not say that a failure after restricted preparation is not a failure of the design. The addendum's distinction (Check A = cold-read diagnostic with its preparation recorded; Check B = performance after the actual unit sequence, inside ordinary teaching) is the correct repair and costs nothing.
- **ACTFL Alignment P-4 already requires this.** "When real learners are measured, the empirical result supersedes the estimate, on the record" (§1, binding since SC-048) and §5.3 "learner-work calibration once the level has students". The standard gives P-4 its procedure; it does not change the framework.

One correction to the package, minor: the addendum's §6 table says the listening fixture tests "were independently run" — they were run by the gate inside this Text session (`verify_report_v46.md`), not by a second party. The evidence record states it that way.

## 3 · The one decision the owner must make

**What closes Unit 1 — the panel, or learners?** SC-121 and the protocol must say the same thing. Two readings are available; each is lawful.

**Route A — panel closes the design; learner evidence is a separate, visible status (recommended, and the auditor's recommendation).** Unit 1 is called complete when the 72 clips are bound, the three photographs are in their slots, and the expert panel has run the check and recorded a design review. The evidence record then reads: technical verified · design review run · learner performance **not observed**. No outward wording may claim observed learning until a learner sitting is recorded. This is SC-009 applied as written, with the honesty the auditor asks for added on top. It does not re-create the classroom-pilot barrier SC-009 removed.

**Route B — learners close the unit.** "Complete" requires Check A and Check B run with children (three to six, ages 8–13), the delayed probe included; the panel may review the design but cannot close the unit. This reverses SC-009 for Unit 1 only. It is the stronger claim and the slower path, and it depends on a room, a teacher and a date that no session can supply.

Under either route the review-build chip stays until closure, and under either route the evidence status is displayed separately from the technical and design statuses (standard §8). The Text role's recommendation is Route A, because it is consistent with the owner's existing rulings and keeps the empirical question open on the record instead of closing it by fiat.
