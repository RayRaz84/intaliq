# Learning-effectiveness governance: handoff and adoption

12 October 2026. Prepared by the instructional auditor at the owner's request: “we need to create governance sources to ensure learning effectiveness too.” **Draft package, not active law.** No governing register entry has been appended and no published content changed.

## Package

1. `INTALIQ1_LEARNING_EFFECTIVENESS_STANDARD.md`: course-wide evidence requirements, claim limits and revision rules.
2. `INTALIQ1_LEARNING_EVIDENCE_TEMPLATE.md`: reusable unit plan, administration record, observations, evidence ledger and revision decision.
3. `INTALIQ1_UNIT1_LEARNING_CHECK_ADDENDUM.md`: extension of the existing Unit 1 check, including actual input exposure, both stories, four skills, delayed performance and bounded transfer.

The sources are operational governance proposals grounded in the current project instruments. They are not a literature review and do not claim research-validated cutoffs or guaranteed effectiveness.

## Owner policy choice, made concrete

**Recommended:** retain expert-panel approval as the release route, but reserve claims of observed learning, retention and transfer for actual learner evidence. Unit 1 may be published as a review build while that evidence is pending. Clarify whether its “complete” label requires learners under SC-121 or may be closed by the panel under SC-009; whichever route is adopted, display the evidence status separately and honestly.

This avoids a new universal classroom-pilot launch barrier while ensuring a panel-only release is not described as empirically effective. A guarantee that all learners will acquire the language is not an available evidence claim.

## Acceptance conditions for governance integration

- Every unit can name its communicative function, preparation, allowed supports, repeated processing and later reuse.
- Every evidence claim points to actual observations and a version.
- Reading and listening are reported separately; scaffold-supported writing is not labeled free production.
- Panel-only records cannot produce an observed-learning label.
- Partial delivery, missing assets and extra assistance remain visible in interpretation.
- Delayed and transfer evidence can remain pending without fabrication or a misleading pass.
- A detected instructional failure leads to a source change and recheck record.
- No new authority conflict, language permission, product score system or learner-data practice is silently created.
