# Unit Generation Runbook — version 0

*Created 2026-09-15 under SC-078 · Editor · **prospective, not retrospective***

```
WHAT VERSION 0 IS
A hypothesis about how a unit should be built, written BEFORE Unit 2 so that Unit 2 can test it.
It is not a record of anything that has happened. Unit 1 was retrofitted to the instruments,
not generated by them, so no stage below is yet known to work.

Every deviation found during Unit 2 is logged in §17 as a TRIAL FINDING and kept separate from
the prescribed steps. After Unit 2 passes its gate, §17 is folded in and this becomes version 1.
Until then this runbook governs nothing: it is the thing being tested.
```

**Standing authority for every stage:** Editorial Constitution → ACTFL alignment → Alhawary (grammar) → Buckwalter & Parkinson (lexis) → Two-Story Blueprint / scope map → Vocabulary Architecture → Recycling Map → Activity Architecture. A decision becomes operational only when recorded in the Decision Register (**SC-071**).

**Standing escalation rule (SC-078).** Inside an approved Gate A brief, craft judgement is the Editor's. Escalate only to admit unapproved core language · expand a paradigm · violate or amend a hard band · alter a course-wide architectural rule · contradict a higher-authority instrument · require a new exception.

## The fifteen stages

### 1 · Load governing instruments
**Inputs** the eight instruments above, plus the Decision Register read forward from the last unit. **Authority** SC-071. **Operation** confirm each is at its current version and that no entry since the last unit supersedes a rule this unit will rely on. **Output** a one-page currency note naming each instrument, its last amendment and the register entry that made it. **Pass** no instrument carries an unmarked supersession; no two active instruments state contradictory rules on anything this unit will use. **Fail →** a reconciliation item, resolved before stage 2. **Register** none unless a conflict is found.

### 2 · Resolve unit scope and outcomes
**Inputs** scope map entry for this unit · ACTFL alignment · exit profile · previous unit's frozen state. **Authority** scope map (adopted SC-048); ACTFL alignment §6. **Operation** state the communicative affordance, the headline structural event, and the per-mode performance targets. **Output** scope statement, **naming which story رَزَان centres (SC-079)**. **Pass** exactly one headline structural event (§19); Razanne centres one story; affordance traceable to the scope map; targets stated per mode, never as proficiency claims. **Fail →** stage 1 if an instrument conflict caused it; otherwise revise here. **Register** none.

### 3 · Prepare the Gate A brief
**Inputs** stage 2 output · both ledgers · Chunk Inventory · Recycling Map · Cast Bible · Blueprint engine and shape catalogue. **Authority** `INTALIQ1_GATE_A_TEMPLATE.md`. **Operation** fill every mandatory field; where evidence is missing, do the lookup rather than deferring it. **Output** a completed brief. **Pass** every mandatory field present; every proposed item carries frequency evidence or a recorded rationale; no field reads "to be confirmed". **Fail →** the missing evidence work, then back here. **Register** the brief is proposed, not recorded, until approved. **← THE ONLY OWNER APPROVAL IN THE PIPELINE.**

### 4 · Reserve resources
**Inputs** approved brief. **Authority** Vocabulary Architecture V1–V22 · Recycling Map · Chunk Inventory · Cast Bible. **Operation** write the reservations into the ledgers as *proposed*: new core, recycled, support, recognition; licensed grammatical forms; chunks with their permissions; cast assignments; recycling debts owed to earlier units. **Output** reservation block. **Pass** nothing reserved that the brief did not authorize; every recycling debt from the previous two units' headline structures is on the list (LC-3). **Fail →** stage 3 if the brief is short; otherwise fix here. **Register** none — reservations are proposals until the unit freezes.

### 5 · Verify Story 1's present-time permissions
**Inputs** reservation block. **Authority** **SC-073 item 1**. **Operation** list the present/imperfect forms, nominal and existential patterns and dialogue structures Story 1 may use, each traced to a Grammar Ledger row with subsection and printed page. **Output** Story 1 language envelope. **Pass** every listed form has a citation; **no form is admitted that would require the imperfect to do pseudo-past work**. **Fail →** stage 4. **Register** none.

### 6 · Verify Story 2's exact past-form permissions
**Inputs** reservation block. **Authority** **SC-073 items 2 and 3**; bank **B-PA6**; the SC-065 form guard. **Operation** name **each** past form Story 2 may print, with its Grammar Ledger row, subsection and printed page — **and name the forms explicitly not authorized** (other persons, genders, numbers, roots, attached forms). **Output** two lists: authorized, not authorized. **Pass** every authorized form cited; the not-authorized list is non-empty and explicit; no form is admitted because a related form is licensed. **Fail →** stage 4, or escalate if a needed form is unlicensed. **Register** none.

### 7 · Declare three differences beyond tense
**Inputs** brief · Blueprint rule 22 list. **Authority** **SC-074**. **Operation** name **three** differences from *protagonist · problem · story engine · setting · object · information structure · emotional tone · ending · perspective · type of interaction*, and say how each will be realized. **Output** differentiation declaration. **Pass** three named, each from the list, each realized rather than asserted. **A protagonist difference counts only if role, goal, knowledge, relationships, choices or experience of the problem differ — a different name does not count (SC-079).** Two stories losing different objects do not by that fact have different problems; moving the same information between narration and dialogue is not by itself an information-structure difference; and the two stories may not be one event chain in two tense frames. **The tense contrast does not count.** Superficial substitution of names, objects, quantities or locations does not satisfy a slot, and required lexical redistribution does not count as a narrative difference. **Fail →** stage 3 if the brief's engines are too close; otherwise redesign here. **Register** none.

### 8 · Draft and audit Story 1
**Inputs** stages 5 and 7. **Authority** Blueprint (30 rules) · Story Standard (what the Blueprint does not cover) · Story Craft Conditions · SC-070's existential principle. **Operation** draft, then audit against: the language envelope · the Blueprint's rule-28 gate · LC-6 length-follows-load · the وَ audit · banned features · SC-027 and SC-036 guards. **Output** Story 1, fully vocalized, with a machine-counted word count dated to the moment it is stated (**SC-050**). **Pass** every audit item passes; every word is in the envelope; the count reconciles with the load. **Fail →** stage 8 (redraft), or stage 4 if the envelope genuinely cannot say what the story needs. **Register** none until freeze.
**⟨SC-091/SC-093 amendment⟩** Before drafting: the one-sentence situation with no Arabic, then the design card with CR-6/9/10/12 fields. **Verbs-first check (CR-4):** every line tried with existing verbs before a support word enters; record the prior-unit verb count. Length regime CR-3 (Story 1 120–250). Blocked language enters as support/input-first and goes into the disclosure table (SC-090).


**Amendment ⟨SC-098⟩.** Each story is titled at drafting, on its design card, as a storybook would title it — the question the story asks or its central object and people — never the grammar target or a vocabulary item. The title is confirmed at Gate B and is the `title` field of the engine unit.

### 9 · Draft and audit Story 2
**Inputs** stages 6 and 7; Story 1 as frozen. **Authority** as stage 8, plus **SC-073 items 2 and 5**. **Operation** draft as an **independent narrative**, then audit as stage 8 plus: every printed past form is on the authorized list · nonpast forms appear only where natural Arabic requires them · the three declared differences are realized. **Output** Story 2 with its own counted length. **Pass** as above; **and the story is not Story 1 converted verb by verb** — the test is whether its events could be told in the present without becoming Story 1. **Fail →** stage 9 or stage 7. **Register** none until freeze.
**⟨SC-091 amendment⟩** Story 2 is a different story (rule 22), 80–150 words, turn-driven; retellings of Story 1 go to S11/S10, never here (CR-11). Antecedent check for every attached pronoun.

### 10 · Update ledgers and recycling records
**Inputs** both frozen stories. **Authority** Architecture §11 · §26.5 · §26.6 · §26.7 · Recycling Map. **Operation** convert reservations to entries: lemma, printed forms attested in each reading, status, evidence; grammar rows with citations; chunk rows with permissions; recycling obligations created for later units. **Output** updated ledgers. **Pass** every printed form appears on exactly one card's `forms` array; no card claims an unprinted form; no lemma is counted twice; chunks counted separately from lemmas. **Fail →** here. **Register** entry naming what was added.
**⟨SC-091/SC-093 amendment⟩** Also produce the **recombination page** (S10, 8–12 texts), the **Version A/B** strips (S11), the **phrase page** (B0) and this unit's additions to the **personal-question set** (A9). Ledger rows for support items may read *lookup owed*; never an estimated rank. **The roll check (Cast Bible §5) is removed** — there is no roll (SC-089).

### 11 · Generate activities
**Inputs** frozen stories · updated ledgers. **Authority** Activity Architecture (closed catalog) · **SC-032** six sets per reading · **SC-025** independence · **SC-048(7)** no scores or telemetry. **Operation** instantiate from the catalog only; no unit invents a format. **Output** activity sets plus the platform data. **Pass** every activity is a catalog instance; every prompt is a phrase or sentence, never a bare word (**SC-038**); no activity targets a `G-orthographic_presence` structure; distractors are meaningful. **Fail →** here. **Register** none.


**Amendment ⟨SC-119 · SC-120⟩.** The phase order the activities serve is input → reading → **consolidation and scaffolded interaction (C1)** → notebook output → fuller communication and transfer; the survey's questions address the classmate directly and every survey column has a question that elicits it; N2 uses the survey's names and names the printed-roster alternative for an absent learner. **Response resources are part of the format**: a match task prints the pictures it matches to (lettered or numbered), a who-says task prints the faces (numbered), a sequence task prints the lines verbatim — and the who-says and sequence sets are **generated from the engine's `whosays` and `events` tables** by the build, never hand-copied (I2). No English learner instruction outside the pop-up; response rubrics are Arabic chrome, recorded (SC-046).

**Amendment ⟨SC-096⟩.** The notebook (N1–N4, S6, C1) and the retell formats (C4, S11) are generated at the unit's **ladder rung** (brief field 4c; `SCHOOL_COURSE_OUTPUT_LADDER_SCHEDULE.md` §3). Every N1 slot and bank word is verified by script against the canon before freeze, and every notebook page is audited for picture–instruction fit (SC-100): each printed frame is the frame its line describes; each drawing prompt names what the activity asks.

### 12 · Run quality and measurement gates
**Inputs** everything above. **Authority** Blueprint rule 28 · LC-2 recycled share · LC-6 encounter floors · `canonCheck` and the form guard · the unit quality-gate template. **Operation** run every gate and record numerator, denominator and method for the recycled share. **Output** a quality-gate record. **Pass** recycled share **70–85%** measured by script on final prose; **every core item ≥8 encounters counted across all canonical materials, not the platform alone**; canon reproduces the printed text word for word; 0 suppressed activities. **Fail →** stage 13. **Register** none.
**⟨SC-091/SC-093 amendment⟩ Gates:** Blueprint 28 · Standard criteria · CR rules · **CR-1** (Story 2 vs prior units + Story 1 + phrase page ≥80% by lemma) · **CR-2** floors across stories + S10 + activities · `canonCheck` · form guard · dignity · antecedent check. **⟨SC-120⟩** The engine's `deliverable()` must be true for every unit: canon, form, **form licence** (`FORM_LICENCE`), **reading membership** (every sentence, in order, every id resolving), orphan and audio-flag checks are unit-level and **withhold the unit**; the build refuses on any of them. Encounter reports distinguish **print · generated · selected-pathway** columns (Recycling Map, Unit 1 report). **Reports, not gates:** Story 1 share vs prior units · band overrun · support count · owed-lookup count · name density · words per slot. The former flat LC-2 gate is withdrawn.

### 13 · Revise failures
**Inputs** the gate record. **Authority** the failing instrument. **Operation** repair at the smallest scope that fixes the cause — an out-of-band reading returns to drafting with **genuine new content, never padding**; an encounter shortfall is repaired by the smallest natural addition using already licensed forms. **Output** revised artifacts. **Pass** re-run stage 12 clean. **Fail →** escalate under SC-078 if the repair would need unapproved language. **Register** none.
**⟨SC-090 amendment⟩** SC-078's six escalation triggers are **disclosure** triggers: a trigger met is written into the disclosure table and seen at Gate A/B; only the untouched list (sensitivity law, dignity, tense slots, evidence rules, register) still halts.

### 14 · Freeze the candidate unit
**Inputs** passing artifacts. **Authority** SC-050 · SC-064's canon guard. **Operation** write the printed text into each unit's `canon`; re-extract the engine; republish; mirror every instrument to the project. **Output** a frozen unit. **Pass** `canonCheck` passes for both readings; every stated count re-run at the moment it is stated. **Fail →** stage 13. **Register** the freeze entry, naming every instrument it cascaded to.

**Amendment ⟨SC-104⟩.** The freeze also produces the unit's **teacher's page**: run `tools/build_teacher_pages.py` after the engine is re-extracted (it reads the counts from the engine and refuses to build on a failing unit); add the unit's flow entries, its C3 example questions and any unit-specific note to the generator's data; republish the teacher's page as its **own artifact**. The generator asserts that the page carries no answers and that the Studio does not link it — both assertions must hold or the freeze does not pass.

**Amendment ⟨SC-120⟩.** The freeze is one command: `INTALIQ_STUDIO_VERSION=<n> node source/pipeline/build_studio_art.cjs`, which refreshes Unit 2's declarations, **embeds `engine_core.js` wholesale into the Studio** (the Studio's engine is never edited by hand), regenerates every `<!-- gen:… -->` block from the engine's data, verifies the Unit 2 art inventory and hashes, runs the teacher-page generator and writes `index.html`, `404.html` and `teacher.html`. Then `verify_release.py <deploy> <n>` (TF-9, extended) must exit 0. A change made only to a generated file is overwritten by the next build.

### 15 · Record deviations and improve the runbook
**Inputs** everything that happened. **Authority** SC-078. **Operation** write each deviation into §17 below as a numbered trial finding: what the runbook said, what actually had to happen, and whether the runbook or the instrument was wrong. **Output** §17 entries. **Pass** every deviation logged; **none silently folded into the prescribed stages**. **Register** an entry naming the findings.

---

## 16 · What this runbook does not do

It does not decide scope, bands, or status. It does not authorize language. It does not replace the Gate A approval, which is the single human decision in the pipeline. And it does not claim that following it produces a good unit — only that it produces an auditable one.
