The unit flow five phases, in the order the course requires (meaning → reading → consolidation and scaffolded interaction → output → fuller communication and transfer; SC-119)

  1. 1
    InputThe learner, alone
    • Digital · الْمُفْرَدَات — every core item met, recognised and met again in a sentence (48 tasks, planned sequence).
    • Textbook · اُنْظُرْ! — the labelled phrase scene (B1) and the six-frame strip.

    Shared devices at the top of class, or the picture spread where there are none. Do not explain the words; the pictures and phrases do that. Sound is the browser's preview voice on request only — recorded audio is not yet verified, so nothing here is a listening task.

  2. 2
    ReadingThe learner (the class may read together)
    • Reading 1 «قَائِمَةُ رَزَان» — 138 words, present narration — then its sets: اِقْرَأْ وَصِلْ · صَحٌّ أَمْ خَطَأٌ؟ أَرِنِي · مَنْ يَقُولُ؟ · اِخْتَرْ وَاكْتُبْ · رَتِّبْ · اِبْحَثْ فِي النَّصِّ. Digital · الْقِرَاءَة ١ (46 tasks selected from 128 generated).
    • Reading 2 «مَنْ أَنْتَ يَا قَلَم؟» — 71 words, past narration, dialogue in the present — then its sets, ending with الْآنَ أَمْ قَبْلُ؟ (sort each line into story 1 or story 2 — the titles are the categories). Digital · الْقِرَاءَة ٢ (28 tasks selected from 78 generated).

    Readings are read cold — there is no oral pre-telling in this course. Every set is self-checking against the page it sits on; nothing to mark.

  3. 3
    Consolidation and scaffolded interactionThe learner; then the class, with you facilitating
    • The pop-up «Look — no “is”!» — the book's only English (LC-4: one per unit, no rule drilled).
    • قَائِمَةُ الصَّفِّ — the class roll as functional text (اِسْتَعْمِلِ النَّصَّ), with the named row of faces; أَسْتَطِيعُ! — the can-do page the learner ticks by saying the lines.
    • C1 · اِسْأَلْ أَصْدِقَاءَكَ — notebook p. 9: «مَنْ أَنْتَ؟ / مَنْ أَنْتِ؟» · «هَلْ عِنْدَكَ / عِنْدَكِ قَلَمٌ؟» · «هَلْ عِنْدَكَ / عِنْدَكِ قَائِمَةٌ؟» — rehearsed from the printed line, then asked of five real classmates. Its names feed p. 5.

    Scaffolded interaction comes BEFORE notebook writing (owner's decision, SC-119): run C1 here, not after the notebook. A learner who missed it completes p. 5 from the printed class list instead.

  4. 4
    OutputThe learner, alone — begun in class, finishable at home
    • Notebook p. 2–3 · اُكْتُبِ الْقِصَّةَ: «قَائِمَةُ رَزَان» — six frames, one line per frame, bank printed (Captions rung, SC-096).
    • Notebook p. 4 · اُكْتُبِ الْقِصَّةَ: «مَنْ أَنْتَ يَا قَلَم؟» — three frames; the past forms are copied from the bank, never built.
    • Notebook p. 5–6 · قِصَّتِي الْمُوَازِيَةُ — the same story about the learner's real class, using the names collected in C1.
    • Notebook p. 7 · قِصَّتِي — starters, printed models for every choice, a split bank (things / people / a line to say). p. 8 · دَوْرِي — the name poster, which prepares the share.
    • Notebook p. 10 · هَلْ تَذْكُرُ؟ — blank at Unit 1 by design (it draws on earlier units only).

    Every page here is completable alone (P-3). Do not mark; the pages are shown, not graded.

  5. 5
    Fuller communication and transferThe teacher facilitates — zero prep, no props
    • C3 · هٰذِهِ صَفْحَتِي! — the last line of p. 6, and the poster on p. 8: show a partner, who asks one question.
    • C4 · نُعِيدُ الْقِصَّةَ بِالصُّوَرِ — printed under the six-frame strip in the book; run it here, after both readings, not when the strip is first met.
    • Transfer — the same language in a new small situation: a classmate's pen, a list of another class.

    Everything here runs on pages the unit has already produced. Nothing is made, cut or printed. At the Captions rung you cue each frame of C4 and the class supplies the language.

Procedures the three teacher-run formats; three lines each

C1اِسْأَلْ أَصْدِقَاءَكَAsk your friends — the survey (Phase 3, before the notebook)
  1. Notebook open at p. 9. Read the printed questions aloud once, in Arabic, and the two answer models («عِنْدِي…» ✓ · «لَيْسَ عِنْدِي…» ✗); do not translate them.
  2. Learners stand, ask five classmates — the name, the pen, the list — write each name and tick ✓ or ✗ in each column.
  3. Stop when every grid has five names. Those names are used on p. 5, so this runs before the parallel story; a learner who missed it uses the printed class list on p. 5.
C3هٰذِهِ صَفْحَتِيShare your page
  1. In pairs. The learner holds up the finished page (p. 5–6 or the poster on p. 8) and says the printed line: «هٰذِهِ صَفْحَتِي!»
  2. The partner points to one thing on the page and asks one question from the unit's own set — for this unit, e.g. «مَنْ هٰذَا؟» · «هَلْ عِنْدَكَ قَلَمٌ؟».
  3. Swap roles. If there is a wall, the pages go on it; nothing is marked.
C4نُعِيدُ الْقِصَّةَStory circle — the retell (Phase 5, after both readings)
  1. Books open at the strip. Point to frame 1 and wait; the class says what happens — at the Captions rung you cue the frames, the class supplies the language.
  2. Write on the board only what the class says, as they say it. Do not supply or correct toward the printed text.
  3. Move to the next frame once a sentence has been said for this one. The story ends when the strip does.

Pilot notes what the classroom pilot must report (Activity Architecture §8) — write here, or on paper

  1. Of the two readings, how much could learners retell from the strip without the text? (a rough share, and which frames stalled)

  2. First cold read of Reading 1: what did the class understand before the phrase page and strip, and what changed after them?

  3. Were the personal questions (وَأَنْتَ؟ وَأَنْتِ؟) answered without you? Which ones needed you?

  4. Was the recombination page read, skipped, or read once? (Unit 2 onward)

  5. On the notebook: did learners complete p. 5–6 and p. 7 unassisted? Where did they stop and ask?

  6. Anything a learner said in Arabic that the unit did not teach — write it down exactly.
