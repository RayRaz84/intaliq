**What this is for, in the document's own words:** *"The map exists to prevent accidental vocabulary disappearance. Do not distort a story merely to satisfy the table."* Planned returns are **commitments to check**, not predictions. A planned cell that a later unit cannot fill naturally is moved, not forced.

**How to read the columns.** *Introduced* = the unit and story where the item first appears in print. *Same-unit* = every channel inside its own unit, measured not estimated. *Next-unit* / *Later* = planned return, marked ⟨planned⟩ until a real unit fills it. *Productive?* = whether the learner is eventually expected to produce it, or only understand it.
