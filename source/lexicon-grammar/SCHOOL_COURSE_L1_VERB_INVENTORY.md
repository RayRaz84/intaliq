# The School Course — Level 1 Verb Inventory (SC-094)

*Owner ruling, 2026-09-16: the owner's list of thirty-three first-person verbs is **adopted** as the Level 1 verb inventory, minus the three the deferred-constructions list already holds; **each unit must have multiple target verbs**; the synonym pairs for *live*, *return* and *do* are **all adopted and distributed across units**, not chosen between. This file is the inventory and its distribution. The scope map, the ten-unit progression and the curriculum map carry the same distribution ⟨SC-094⟩; the register carries the ruling.*

---

## 1 · Definitions

**Target verb.** A verb made **productive** in its home unit: third person singular present and past, masculine and feminine, each past form licensed at that unit's Gate A with Alhawary subsection and printed page (SC-073); the **first-person form licensed in dialogue and in the personal-question set from the unit of first exposure** (Blueprint rule 10; CR-16) — the old rule that held all `أَنَا` forms for Unit 5 is superseded for dialogue; Unit 5 still teaches the present-tense system and Unit 8 the past system.

**Exposure before home.** A verb may appear as a **story verb in support** in any unit before its home unit (CR-6, SC-090), disclosed, never assessed there.

**The verb track.** Target verbs are counted on their **own track, 3–6 per unit**, separate from the 3–5 noun-and-function-word band; the two bands report together at Gate A (§23 of the template). This is the architectural consequence of the ruling and is recorded as such.

**Evidence.** No rank is recorded here. Every verb below is **lookup owed** in the frequency dictionary except the three already evidenced (`أَرَادَ` 126 / p. 20 · `دَخَلَ` 297 / p. 194 · `تَكَلَّمَ` 926 / p. 129). Alhawary citations follow the verb's form class: sound Form I 13.4.1 p. 189 · geminate 13.4.2 p. 192 · hollow 13.4.5 p. 197 · defective 13.4.6 p. 203 · assimilated (`وَجَدَ · وَضَعَ`) 13.4.4 · hamzated (`أَخَذَ · أَتَى · سَأَلَ · بَدَأَ · قَرَأَ`) 13.4.3 · Forms II–X 13.4.x — each **to verify by eye** at the home unit's Gate A.

**Orthography settled by this file:** `آتِي` (not `أَأْتِي`) · `أَفْهَمُ` · `أُعْطِي` takes two objects (`أُعْطِيهِ كِتَابًا`); `أُعْطِي لِـ` is not printed.

**Deferred with the constructions they need (unchanged):** `أَسْتَطِيعُ` (needs `أَنْ` + subjunctive) · `أُصْبِحُ` · `لَا أَزَالُ` (sisters of `كَانَ`) — Level 2.
