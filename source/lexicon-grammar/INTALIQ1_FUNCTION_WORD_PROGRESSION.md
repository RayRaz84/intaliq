## 2 · Grammatical classification — Alhawary's terminology, with citations

Read off the page image 2026-09-15. **The single most consequential correction: the items the project has been calling "locative prepositions" are not prepositions in Alhawary's scheme.**

| Class | Alhawary § | printed p. | Items in this course |
|---|---|---:|---|
| **Preposition** (حرف جر) | **6.1** Basic Meanings and Use of Prepositions | **119** | `فِي` (6.1.5, p. 122) · `عَلَى` (6.1.4, p. 121) · `لِـ` (6.1.9, p. 124) · `مَعَ` (6.1.11, p. 125) · `بِـ` (6.1.8, p. 123) |
| **Adverb of place** (ظرف مكان) | **8.2** Adverbs of Place | **147** | `تَحْتَ` · `فَوْقَ` · `أَمَامَ` · `خَلْفَ` · `جَانِبَ` · `هُنَا` — *"take the accusative case ending"*, and the section's own list prints تَحْتَ "under", فَوْقَ "over", أَمَامَ "in front of", خَلْفَ "behind", جَانِبَ "next to" |
| **Word used as adverb of time or place** | **8.3** Words Used as Adverbs of Time or Place | **148** | **`عِنْدَ` "at"**, listed there beside `قَبْلَ` and `بَعْدَ` |
| **Interrogative locative** | **7.1.8** ’ayna "Where" Question Word | **138** | `أَيْنَ` |
| **Interrogative of cause** | **7.1.6** limaadhaa "Why" Question Word | **137** | `لِمَاذَا` |
| **Coordinating conjunction** | citation due at its promotion unit | — | `وَ` · `أَوْ` |
| **Contrastive connector** | citation due at U8 | — | `لٰكِنْ` / `وَلٰكِنْ` |
| **Causal connector** | citation due at U7 | — | `لِأَنَّ` |
| **Additive adverb — not a conjunction** | citation due at U4 | — | `أَيْضًا` (B&P lemmatizes under `أَيْض`, Manual §4.4d) |
| **Sequencing connector** | citation due at its unit | — | `ثُمَّ` |

**Three consequences the project must carry.**

**`تَحْتَ` and `أَمَامَ` are adverbs, not prepositions.** Unit 2's headline is therefore *"three prepositions"* only if it means `فِي · عَلَى` plus one adverb of place. The accurate wording is **two prepositions and one adverb of place, plus an interrogative locative and a locative adverb** — and the brief's §3 label should read *locative expressions*, not *locative prepositions*.

**`عِنْدَ` is not a preposition either.** It is 8.3's adverb of time-or-place, and its **possessive** use is idiomatic — documented inside B&P's own entry (Manual §4.6), which is why the possessive reading was ever defensible. **`لِـ` by contrast is a true preposition (6.1.9)**, which is why the two do different work and why Unit 1 teaches them as separate items rather than variants.

**Adverbs of place take the accusative.** `تَحْتَ الْكِتَابِ` prints a fatḥa on تَحْتَ and a kasra on الْكِتَابِ. Both fall under **`G-orthographic_presence`** (SC-075): printed correctly, never explained, never assessed.

## 4 · The repairs

### 4.1 · `وَ` — status declared

**It had none.** `وَ` is printed **twenty times** in Unit 1's two readings, carried on ten hosts (`وَعِنْدَهَا` · `وَتَقُولُ` · `وَهُنَاكَ` · `وَمَنْ` · `وَأَنَا` · `وَلِي` · `وَأَنْتِ` · `وَقَالَ` and the two greeting chunks), and it has **no item card, no ledger row, no status and no recorded rank.**

**Declared:** `وَ` is **input-first glue from Unit 1**, exactly as `الـ` is — the scope map's own phrase for Unit 1 is *"الـ admitted as glue."* **No productive objective, no activity target, not assessed**, until its promotion at **U8** alongside `وَلٰكِنْ`, where coordination and contrast are taught together because that is when narration needs both.

**It gets no separate item card**, and the reason is a rule already on the books: Manual §3.2 — *"the article `الـ` and attached clitics are **not** counted as independent tokens: a word is counted once, in the classification of its lexical host."* `وَ` is a proclitic, and the `forms` arrays already carry `وَعِنْدَهَا`, `وَلِي` and the rest. **Carding it would double-count every host.** What was missing was the declaration, not the card.

**SC-035's وَ-audit stands unchanged** — `وَ` only where the connection is real, no decorative sentence-openers.

### 4.2 · The spatial set — three units, not one

The old plan grouped six spatial expressions in one unit. **Rejected**: six adverbs of place at once is precisely the overload the instruction warns against.

| Unit | Spatial items | Why here |
|---|---|---|
| **U2** | `أَيْنَ` · `فِي` · `عَلَى` · `تَحْتَ` · `هُنَا` | the question and the three commonest relations — in, on, under. Enough for a locative story, inside the 3–5 band |
| **U3** | `أَمَامَ` · `بِجَانِبِ` — **candidates, not allocations (SC-084)** | proposed on affordance grounds; **admission, status and count are Unit 3 Gate A's to decide** against the 3–5 band, R-8, total productive load and evidence. Neither may be described as approved new core |
| **never** | `فَوْقَ` · `خَلْفَ` | **not admitted anywhere in Intaliq 1.** `فَوْقَ` duplicates `عَلَى` for a Novice, `خَلْفَ` duplicates `أَمَامَ` inverted. Neither earns a slot, and adding them would be thematic completeness, which the rules forbid |

**`تَحْتَ` and `أَمَامَ` are each introduced exactly once.** The readers' §6 placements do not govern Intaliq.
**`بِجَانِبِ` carries a hard debt: it has no verified rank and may not be a B&P headword.** Under **SC-084** it is **ineligible for core authorization until it has admissible evidence under LC-1** — the U3 brief either closes the lookup or drops it.

### 4.3 · `عِنْدَ` — already correct, confirmed not repaired

Intaliq's U3 wording is already *"U3 finishes what U1 started"* and B-PA5 already marks U1's use **input-first only**. The table records **first exposure U1 · new core U1 (affirmative chunks) · productive promotion U3**. Nothing was duplicated and nothing needed changing.

### 4.4 · `لِـ` and `عِنْدَ` are not the same class

`لِـ` is a **preposition** (6.1.9, p. 124); `عِنْدَ` is an **adverb of time-or-place** (8.3, p. 148) whose possessive reading is idiomatic. Unit 1 teaches them as two items doing two jobs — people versus things — and the grammar backs that split. **Recorded so no later unit collapses them into "the possession prepositions."**

### 4.5 · `مَعَ` — allocated

Unallocated in both products' tables. **Proposed as a U3 candidate (SC-084 — not an allocation)**, because U3's new-core domain is the family set: *"with my mother", "with my friend"* is the affordance a family unit wants, and `مَعَ` **17 / p. 10** is the third-commonest content word in the corpus. It is **canonical NEW (Manual §2.3)** and U3's Gate A is its named approval.

### 4.7 · Units 9 and 10 — one consolidated recommendation

**Recommendation: U9 and U10 introduce no new connector.**

Three reasons, and none of them is capacity. **The functional set closes at U8** — coordination, location, addition, sequencing, alternatives, cause, contrast is the full inventory a Novice narration needs, and the next functions up (concession, condition, purpose, relative linking) all require syntax the deferred-constructions list reserves for Level 2. **A learner who has met eight connectors across eight units has not yet *owned* them**; U9 and U10 are where the earlier ones become productive across longer texts, which is worth more than a ninth item. And **U9 and U10 are not designed** (SC-063 locked the count; SC-048 designs eight), so assigning connectors to them now would design them by the back door — inventing the pedagogy of a unit whose entry profile real Band 5–6 students have not yet revealed.

**What U9 and U10 owe instead:** every connector from U1–U8 recycled in extended narration, and the U8 promotion of `وَ` exercised across clause chains. **If a later U9/U10 design genuinely needs one more, the first candidate is `حَتَّى` or a purpose frame — and that is a decision for those units' Gate A, not for this table.**

## 4.8 · SC-084 — the Unit 3 allocations in this file are corrected to candidacies

**§§4.2 and 4.5 above allocated `أَمَامَ`, `بِجَانِبِ` and `مَعَ` to Unit 3 on functional grounds without checking Unit 3's existing load.** The scope map already gives Unit 3 the possession system as its headline **plus the six-noun family set**. Three more new-core items on top would put Unit 3 near **nine**, against a band of three to five — a worse overload than the one Unit 2 was designed to avoid.

**SC-084 therefore demotes all three from allocations to candidacies.** Their evidence records, proposed affordances and future candidacy are untouched; only their *status* changes. **The six family nouns likewise remain candidates pending R-8** — the scope map listing them is not an authorization. **No combination of the three function words and the six nouns is authorized at this stage**, Unit 3 must stay inside the 3–5 band unless a separate exception is recorded, and support or recognition language may not be relabelled to evade it.

**The Unit 3 inventory is not decided here**, and deciding it is Unit 3 Gate A's work, after Unit 2 has tested the runbook.
