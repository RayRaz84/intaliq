# The School Course — CI Calibration Rules (SC-091)

*Owner ruling, 2026-09-16: "Turn what you take into rules." Every rule below is something the Editor said it would take from the two published CI/TPRS samples (Blaine Ray, **Look, I Can Talk!**; Adriana Ramírez, **Teaching / Learning Spanish with Comprehensible Input**, teacher's and student's books), measured and recorded in `INTALIQ1_CI_SAMPLE_CALIBRATION.md`. Each rule names its source, what it binds, and where it lands. Precedence: these rules sit **with** the Story Standard and the Blueprint as quality conditions under SC-090, above the language-control instruments; where a rule below touches a numbered Blueprint or Standard rule, it refines that rule and does not replace it. Nothing here reopens the sensitivity law, dignity, the tense slots, or the evidence rules.*

*Numbering: CR-1 … CR-16. "Binding" means Gate B fails without it; "default" means the drafting session does it unless the reading has a recorded reason not to.*

---

## A · Measurement

**CR-1 · The recycled-share instrument is re-aimed (LC-2).** *Binding.* Story 2 is measured against **prior units + Story 1 + the unit's phrase page** with a target of **≥80% known tokens** (lemma level: an inflected or suffixed form of a known lemma counts as known; proper names excluded; clitics in their host). Story 1 is measured against prior units **as a report only** and never gates. *Source:* Ramírez's stated "80% comprehensible / 20% new," measured at 83.7% by this method; Ray chapters 2–4 at 36–56% for the cross-unit measure. *Lands:* Vocabulary Architecture (LC-2), runbook stage 12, Gate A template §11, production record method. **SC-085 is dissolved by this rule; no ramp.**

**CR-2 · Encounter floors are unit-level and the recombination page counts.** *Binding.* LC-6's floors (≥3 debut, ≥8 with apparatus) are met across Story 1 + Story 2 + the recombination page (CR-10) + activities, in that order of preference; a reading is never lengthened to meet a floor. *Source:* Ray's *Ejercicio 9*. *Lands:* Vocabulary Architecture (LC-6), Activity Architecture.

## B · Length and repetition

**CR-3 · Two length regimes, one per slot.** *Default.* **Story 1** (builds the language, Blueprint rule 1) may run **120–250 words** and repeats each core structure **≥8 times** across affirmative, negative, question, first and second person; narrative restatement (a later paragraph re-saying an earlier fact in the same words) is permitted in Story 1 up to twice. **Story 2** (expands what learners can do) runs **80–150 words**, turn-driven, no restatement. **No reading under 80 words is a story**; under 80 it is a recombination text and goes on the recombination page. *Source:* Ray ch. 1 (92) then 150–200; Ramírez 250–330 with the story noun every 14 words. *Lands:* Blueprint rule 25 (refined), Standard 7 (Story-1 exception recorded), Gate A template.

**CR-4 · Unit verbs first.** *Binding.* Before a support word enters a line, the line is tried with the verbs and frames the course already has. The drafts file records, per story, the count of prior-unit verb tokens. *Source:* the owner's own catch ("you didn't recycle *to see*"), confirmed by Ray's verb economy. *Lands:* runbook stage 8; Gate B.

## C · Story design

**CR-5 · A search story needs four things.** *Binding for any reading with a search surface.* The object is needed **now** (the want is clocked); a helper is **wrong once**; the locatives **increase in precision** (place → surface → position); the ending carries **one character note** that is not explained. *Source:* Ray ch. 3 against Unit 2's rejected drafts. *Lands:* Standard 6/10/11/14 as the concrete test for engines A, E, G.

**CR-6 · Story verbs precede place.** *Binding from Unit 2.* Each unit may carry **up to three story verbs** (third person singular, present and past) and the prepositions they need, as support under SC-090, ahead of the unit that teaches them productively. The first set is *go · come back · give*, with *say · see · enter* from Unit 1. *Source:* both published sequences put *want · have · go · give · say* in stories 1–3; Ramírez puts prepositions of place at story 15 of 16. *Lands:* scope map (a standing note per unit), function-word progression, Gate A template §3.

**CR-7 · Motive may be spoken.** *Default.* When a reading needs a stated reason, `لِأَنَّ` may appear **in dialogue only, once per reading**, as `G-input_first`, with the clause it introduces built entirely from known language; never in narration before Unit 7 teaches it. *Source:* *porque* in the first story of both samples (Ray ch. 1 ×1; Ramírez extendida ×5). *Lands:* Chunk Inventory (a listed dialogue chunk), Grammar Ledger row citing Alhawary at the ledger.

**CR-8 · One earned emotion word per reading.** *Default.* An emotion word (`حَزِين · سَعِيد` and their feminines, as support) may appear **once per reading, after the event that earns it** (Constitution VIII.7), never in place of the event, and **never as the closing line by default** (Blueprint IX.3). Showing (a laugh, a silence, a look) remains the primary instrument. *Source:* Ray ch. 1 (five emotion words, each after its beat); Ramírez's *está feliz* close as the counter-example. *Lands:* Standard 17 (refined), Blueprint 14 (refined).

**CR-9 · Absurdity is expected, not merely licensed.** *Default.* Each unit's pair contains **at least one absurd or bizarre element that is the engine or the turn** of one of its readings (Standard §E-3), never decoration (E-5). *Source:* Ramírez p. 14, "bizarre and peculiar details … stronger connections"; the cat-president carrying a plotless story. *Lands:* Story 1 / Story 2 design cards (a required field: *the absurd element, and what it does*).

**CR-10 · People are introduced on the page.** *Binding, sized by the envelope.* Every story names its people and gives each at least **one detail the reader can picture or predict from** — an age, a trait, a habit, a want — using only language the unit has; the detail grows as the envelope grows (SC-089 clarification). *Source:* Ramírez Day 1: the class supplies names, ages, nationalities, personality traits. *Lands:* Gate A cast field; Gate B.

**CR-11 · Retellings are apparatus, never Story 2.** *Binding.* A noun-swapped or detail-varied retelling of Story 1 (different names, places, one changed object or outcome) is a **legitimate text in the apparatus** — Version A / Version B picture-retells, the recombination page, the platform's parallel frames — and is **never** placed in the Story 2 slot, which must satisfy Blueprint rule 22. *Source:* Ramírez básica → extendida (a second telling); Ray's Versión A/B; Constitution VIII.3. *Lands:* Blueprint rule 22 (refined), Activity Architecture.

**CR-12 · Cast for attribution.** *Default.* Where the sensitivity law allows, a reading's pair is **one protagonist plus a pronoun**, or **two of different kind** (a boy and a girl, a child and an adult, a person and an animal), so that verb morphology carries the speaker; a same-gender pair is chosen only for a story reason, and rule 19 then accepts a name density near 20%. *Source:* Ray ch. 2 (zero names), ch. 3 (one protagonist + *ella*). *Lands:* Blueprint rule 19 (refined), Gate A cast field.

## D · The phrase page and the apparatus

**CR-13 · The phrase page is a reading aid, sized by the reading.** *Binding.* Each reading pair opens with a numbered picture-phrase page (SC-038) listing **every new core, support and input-first item the readings print, as the phrase the story prints**, with a gloss; typically 12–25 items for this course, up to ~40 where a reading needs it; listed items are **not thereby assessable** — status is set by the ledger, not by the page. *Source:* Ray's 13–19-item picture pages; Ramírez's 38–50-item glossed phrase lists. *Lands:* Activity Architecture format B; Gate A template.

**CR-14 · Every unit ships a recombination page.** *Binding.* Eight to twelve one- to three-sentence texts built from the unit's phrase page and prior units, headed by a rubric that says *read, then read again*, explicitly **not** stories, carrying the encounter floors (CR-2) and free to use any character. *Source:* Ray *Ejercicio 9*. *Lands:* Activity Architecture (new S-format), runbook stage 10.

**CR-15 · Picture-only variants and invented answers.** *Binding.* Each Story 1 has **Version A** (same cast, one element changed) and **Version B** (same shape, setting and object changed) as picture-only retell strips; the short-answer set carries the rubric *if the answer is not in the story, invent it* with at least two questions the story does not answer. *Source:* Ray *Ejercicios 4, 6, 7*. *Lands:* Activity Architecture formats S and C; Standard 23/28/29.

**CR-16 · Personal questions, cumulative.** *Binding.* Each unit adds **8–15 second-person questions** («وَأَنْتَ؟ وَأَنْتِ؟») built from its readings' structures to a **cumulative** set that the platform's preview activities ask in every unit thereafter; they are input-tier (spoken, tapped, or answered from the phrase page), never scored. *Source:* Ramírez's PQ, 7–10 minutes daily, cumulative across the year. *Lands:* Activity Architecture format A; Blueprint rule 10 (as a standing routine), rule 23 (the course's substitute for the oral phase under SC-062's option (i)).

## E · Recorded refusals — what the samples do that these rules do not take

The **cruelty engine** (Ray ch. 1's bad boy) — closed under Standard 18. The **open lexicon** (half of each chapter's new words unlisted) — Intaliq lists everything; CR-13 sizes the list, it does not unlist. The **always-happy close** — Blueprint IX.3 stands; CR-8 permits the word, not the default. **Verbatim restatement outside Story 1** — CR-3 confines it. **Translation as the comprehension method** (Ramírez's sentence-by-sentence translating) — the course has no L1 layer in its readings and none is added.
