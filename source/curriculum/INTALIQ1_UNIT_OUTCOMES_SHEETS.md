# Intaliq 1 — Scope and Sequence (the unit outcomes, as the textbook presents them)

*⟨SC-096, second part; reorganized 2026-09-17 on the owner's instruction: "organize the outcomes sheet the way it appears in the textbook."⟩ Part 1 is the chart a student book prints in its front matter — one row per unit, in the order a teacher reads: title and stories · what the learner can do · vocabulary · grammar · reading and activities · writing and speaking. **Units 1 and 2 are as built; Units 3–8 are planned** (scope map, verb inventory, curriculum map) and marked so — their titles come at drafting (SC-098), their word lists and citations are fixed only at each Gate A. Part 2 holds the committee-facing detail behind every row (Unit 2 first, 2026-09-17; Units 1 and 3–8 added 2026-09-26): the ACTFL indicators quoted, the Alhawary citations with verification status, the support vocabulary, and what the unit does not claim. Grammar terms follow Alhawary (2011) with the traditional Arabic term beside each.*
