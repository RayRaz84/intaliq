# Intaliq 1 — Curriculum Map

*Level 1 of the School Course · ages ≈ 8–13 · entry: decodes fully vocalized script (SC-007) · exit: Novice High interpretive reading, Novice Mid–High interpersonal (ACTFL alignment §3) · created 2026-09-16 by the Editor as a **derived view**: the scope map (SC-048, an assessment map per SC-093), the ten-unit progression, the function-word progression, the Blueprint's engine register, the calibration rules (SC-091) and the register are the sources; where they and this map disagree, they win and this map is corrected. **Status is marked per unit and nothing is invented for units that have not reached Gate A** (SC-008): domains are named, word lists are not; engines are recorded only where a unit has chosen them.*

---

## 1 · The shape of the level

**Eight units** (U1–U8; U9–U10 are locked and not designed, SC-063), each built around **one communicative affordance**, **one headline structural event**, and **two stories in fixed tense slots** — Story 1 narrated in the present, Story 2 in the past, from Unit 1 (SC-073). Units are self-contained and counted in units, never weeks. A child may join at any unit (SC-089's no-standing-cast rule and Blueprint R-2 exist for this).

**Method.** Comprehensible input through story (CI/TPRS), calibrated against two published first-year readers (`INTALIQ1_CI_SAMPLE_CALIBRATION.md`) and adapted for readings that are read **cold** — the course has no teacher book (SC-045); the oral phase is classroom practice, not product (SC-062); the substitutes are the phrase page, the picture strip, the audio-first platform formats, the personal-question set and the recombination page, all untested with children, and no study is required to test them (SC-124; Activity Architecture §8).

**Unit anatomy** (the order a learner meets it):

| Step | Component | Format | Governing rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Phrase page** — every new item the readings print, numbered, pictured, as the phrase the story prints | B0 | SC-038; CR-13 |
| 2 | **Picture strip** — meaning before text; no frame reveals what the text withholds | B2 / A5 | Standard 27 |
| 3 | **Story 1** — present tense, 120–250 words, builds the language | reading | Blueprint 1, 6; CR-3 |
| 4 | Comprehension apparatus — read-match · true/false with pointing · sequence · who-says · choose-and-write · text hunts · **short answers with "invent it if it isn't in the story"** | S2–S9 | Activity Architecture; CR-15 |
| 5 | **Version A / Version B** — Story 1's strip with one element changed, retold from pictures | S11 | CR-11, CR-15 |
| 6 | **Story 2** — past tense, 80–150 words, a different story (three substantive differences beyond tense) | reading | Blueprint 22; SC-074; CR-3 |
| 7 | **Recombination page** — 8–12 one-breath texts, *read, then read again* | S10 | CR-2, CR-14 |
| 8 | **Personal questions** — this unit's 8–15 added to the cumulative set, in the preview activities | A9 | CR-16 |
| 9 | Notebook output ladder (captions → frames → chains; scaffold fades across the level) | N | Activity Architecture §3 N |
| 10 | Classroom strands — **B** information gap · **C** real-world text · **D** supported writing | C | scope map strands |

**What is never assessed** (Architecture §9–10; SC-075; SC-090): case and mood endings · `taa' marbuuTa` · anything listed as support, input-first or recognition · attached pronouns before Unit 3 · past forms beyond each unit's licensed list · the `هُنَا / هُنَاكَ` contrast in Unit 2 · anything on a phrase page by virtue of being listed.

## 3 · Progressions that run across the units

**Tense (SC-073).** Every unit: Story 1 present, Story 2 past, dialogue always present. Past forms are licensed **per form** at each Gate A with subsection and printed page: U1 `دَخَلَ · قَالَ · رَأَى` → U2 adds `ذَهَبَ` and the feminines → later units add only what their stories need → U8 makes the system productive, 3sg then `أَنَا`.

**Function words (SC-082, SC-084, SC-092).** `وَ` input-first glue from U1, productive U8 · `إِلَى` U2 (in the going-chunk) · `أَيْضًا` exposed U2, productive U4 · `ثُمَّ` U5 · `أَوْ` U6 · `لِأَنَّ` dialogue-only from U2, productive U7 · `وَلٰكِنْ` U8 · `أَمَامَ · بِجَانِبِ · مَعَ` U3 candidates.

**Pronoun suffixes (SC-090, SC-092).** Singular `ـي · ـكَ · ـكِ · ـهُ · ـهَا` input-first from U2 on nouns, `لِـ` and as objects, off locatives · productive as U3's system · on locatives from U3.

**Story verbs (CR-6).** Up to three per unit as support ahead of U5: U2 `ذَهَبَ` (core) + `رَجَعَ · أَعْطَى · ضَحِكَ`; each later unit names its own at Gate A; U5 systematizes what has been exposed.

**Verbs (SC-094).** Thirty-six verbs across the level on their own track, 3–6 targets per unit, home units per `SCHOOL_COURSE_L1_VERB_INVENTORY.md`: U1 `قَالَ · رَأَى · دَخَلَ · أَحَبَّ · أُرِيدُ` → U2 `ذَهَبَ · رَجَعَ · وَضَعَ` → U3 `أَعْطَى · أَخَذَ · اِحْتَاجَ · عَادَ · عَاشَ` → U4 `أَحَبَّ (1sg) · أَكَلَ · شَرِبَ · نَظَرَ إِلَى` → U5 `قَرَأَ · كَتَبَ · لَعِبَ · سَكَنَ · خَرَجَ · مَشَى · عَمِلَ · بَدَأَ` → U6 `بَحَثَ عَنْ · وَجَدَ · سَأَلَ · عَرَفَ · سَمِعَ · أَتَى` → U7 `شَعَرَ بِـ · بَكَى · ضَحِكَ · فَهِمَ · فَعَلَ` → U8 `تَذَكَّرَ · صَنَعَ` + the past system. Synonym pairs split across units: `رَجَعَ / عَادَ` · `عَاشَ / سَكَنَ` · `عَمِلَ / فَعَلَ`. First-person forms in dialogue and personal questions from first exposure. Deferred to Level 2: `أَسْتَطِيعُ · أُصْبِحُ · لَا أَزَالُ`.

**Emotion (CR-8, U7).** At most one earned emotion word per reading from U2 (`حَزِين / سَعِيد` as support, lookup owed); the domain becomes core in U7; endings are never *everyone is happy* by default.

**Absurdity (E-3, CR-9).** At least one absurd element per unit pair, as engine or turn: U1's talking pen; U2 not yet met.

**People (SC-089, CR-10).** No standing cast; **رَزَان** is the single recurring figure (SC-092). Each unit introduces its own people with one picturable detail, growing as the envelope grows: U2 a name and where their key is; U3 family roles; U7 a feeling and its reason.

**Length (CR-3).** Story 1 120–250 words; Story 2 80–150; nothing under 80 is a story. U1's readings (138 / 71) predate the rule.

**Recycled share (CR-1).** Gate: Story 2 against prior units + Story 1 + phrase page ≥80% known by lemma. Report: Story 1 against prior units. U2: 72% by form / ~90% by lemma (gate met); 33% (report).

**Engine and shape rotation (Blueprint 4–5).** No engine or shape repeats in consecutive units as primary: U1 **B · A+G / Social · Accumulation→Surprise** → U2 **M (G-surface) · K+O / Surprise · Convergence** → U3 must avoid M, K, G, A as primary and Surprise, Convergence as primary shape.

## 4 · ACTFL trajectory (performance, never proficiency)

Per-unit labels are set at each Gate A from task demand, not assigned here. The level's anchors: interpretive reading **Novice High** (supported, fully vocalized text) · listening **Novice Mid–High** (visually supported) · interpersonal **Novice Mid → High** · presentational **Novice Mid**. U1 and U2 briefs carry their per-mode targets; U2's reading target is *emerging Novice High* on the strength of holding three successive location statements about one object. Exit: a learner reads ≈120–150 owned words in connected text, asks and answers where / what / who / why / yes-no, states possession, wants and likes, describes routines, and recounts a simple completed event with learned forms — **Novice High, honestly stated** (ACTFL alignment §3 benchmarking note). The exit performance is Level 2's entry profile ⟨SC-124: the designed exit profile, revisable by design review; no pilot⟩.

## 5 · Standing risks the map carries

The **oral gap** (readings read cold; substitutes untested with children; handled in design — SC-124) · **U3's family-noun flag P-1** · the **U5 verb load** against the story-verb exposure of U2–U4 (the risk is under-load, not over-load, once verbs arrive early) · **owed frequency lookups** (11 for U2; six from U1) · **support drift** under SC-090, watched by the per-unit support count · **Unit 1 as the odd one out** — classroom, standing cast, old band; not reopened, not the model.
