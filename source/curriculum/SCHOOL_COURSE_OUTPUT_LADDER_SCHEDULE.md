# The School Course — Level 1 Output Ladder: the scaffold-fade schedule

*Proposed 2026-09-17 in answer to the owner's question: is there increasing difficulty across the units in the notebook's production activities? **Status: ADOPTED 2026-09-17 ⟨SC-096⟩ — operational.** The Activity Architecture (§3 N, §7) states that scaffolding "fades across the level" and that the schedule is "set at each unit's Gate A"; the curriculum map compresses it to "captions → frames → chains". No Gate A has yet set a rung, and Units 1 and 2 were built at the same tier. This file is the missing schedule.*

---

## 1 · The finding

**Input is graduated; output is not.** Readings lengthen and carry more structures (CR-3), the personal-question set is cumulative (CR-16), N4 reaches further back each unit. But every production format a learner meets in Unit 2 is the format of Unit 1 at the same scaffold: N1 copies one bank word per line; N2 swaps names, an object and a place in a printed skeleton; N3 fills sentence starters from a split bank; the retells (C4 oral, S11 Version A/B, N1 written) are the same task in both units. Left alone, Unit 8 would be Unit 1 with longer texts.

## 2 · The principle

One scaffold is removed per **pair** of units, never per unit, so each rung is met twice — once new, once familiar — matching the two-story-per-unit rhythm. Nothing is ever removed that the readings have not supplied first (the order-is-law rule, Activity Architecture §2). The past tense is the one hard constraint: **past forms are copied from a bank, never built, until Unit 8 teaches the system** (SC-073, verb inventory §2).

## 3 · The schedule

| Rung | Units | N1 · write the story (retell) | N2 · parallel story | N3 · own story | Retells in class and book (C4 · S11) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Captions** | U1–U2 | copy-and-complete: one slot per picture line, bank printed | printed skeleton, 3–4 swaps (names · object · place), C3 line | 4 starters, split bank (things / where / where-to) | C4: retell from the strip, teacher-led; S11: Version A/B picture-only, spot the change |
| **Frames** | U3–U4 | two slots per line; bank printed **but shuffled and over-full** (two distractors) | skeleton keeps only the verbs; the learner supplies every noun phrase and **one whole line** | starters, **no bank** — the learner's own S4 truths and C1 survey data are the source | C4: learner-led, one picture each; S11: Version B **written** as the changed lines only |
| **Chains** | U5–U6 | **no bank**: the strip plus one starter word per line; the learner writes the line from the picture | skeleton reduced to the picture strip + first and last lines; 5–6 lines are the learner's | a 5–6-line chain on the routine set (U5's verbs) — a day, in order | C4: retell without the strip, the class corrects; S11: the learner **invents** the change (CR-15's invent-it, applied to the strip) |
| **Stories** | U7–U8 | the strip only; **U8 (past system home): the learner writes the past forms** — the first page in the level where a past form is built, not copied | the learner rewrites the story with a **different ending** (Story Standard's shape: want → problem → earned or unexpected ending) | a free page on the story shape — a small want, a concrete object, a person who is wrong once, an ending — the Author's Page §2 step 1 handed to the learner | C4: retell in the past (U8); S11: Version C, the learner's own, presented (C3) |

**N4 · Do you remember?** follows its own line: U2 draws on U1 · U3–U4 on the previous unit · U5+ on **two units back**, so that the retrieval interval lengthens as the level goes on.

**S6 · دَوْرِي / C3.** U1–U2 a poster with one line and a drawing · U3–U4 the poster becomes a two-line exchange presented to a partner · U5–U6 the learner presents an N3 page · U7–U8 the learner presents the rewritten story (N2) to the class.

**Units 9–10.** Level 1 is ten units (SC-063); the two reserved units are undesigned by ruling. The ladder above covers the eight designed units; whether U9–U10 extend the Stories rung or add a fifth is decided at their Gate A, from what the pilot shows a Unit 8 learner can write.

## 4 · What this claims, in ACTFL terms

Performance within taught domains only (P-1). Captions and frames are Novice Low–Mid work (lists, memorised phrases, frames completed); chains are Novice Mid–High (strings of sentences on familiar topics); the U7–U8 story page is the first Novice High production task and is where the level's exit claim is tested. The alignment file's §5 calibration should cite this table once it is adopted.

## 6 · Risks

The rungs above are a design, not a measurement. The classroom pilot (Activity Architecture §8) is where the Frames rung gets tested at ages 8–13 — if learners cannot supply a whole line unassisted at U3, the rung moves to U5 and the schedule compresses. The open-items line "confirmation at the panel that N2/N3 frames work unassisted" is the same question and should be answered on the same day.
