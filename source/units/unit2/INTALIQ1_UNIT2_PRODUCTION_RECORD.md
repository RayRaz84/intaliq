## 3 · What fails — LC-2, and it fails structurally

**Method:** token count on final prose per reading; proper names, punctuation and illustration labels excluded; `الـ` and clitics counted in their host (Manual §3.2).

| Reading | Denominator | Recycled | New | **Share** | Band 70–85% |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Story 1 | 61 | 25 | 36 | **41.0%** | **BELOW FLOOR** |
| Story 2 | 41 | 19 | 22 | **46.3%** | **BELOW FLOOR** |

**The type estimate predicted 76.3%. The token measurement is 41%.** The gap is not an error in either figure — it is the difference the owner insisted on when he struck the prediction from revision 2. **A locative story spends its tokens on locatives.** `الصُّورَةُ` alone occurs seven times in Story 1; the five new prepositions and adverbs occur sixteen times between them.

### The repair pass (stage 13), run once

Story 1 was rewritten with genuine recycled material — a full greeting exchange, `أُرِيدُ` stating the want, `عِنْدِي` for possession, `لَيْسَ هُنَا` for the failed check, and a closing `لِي صَدِيقَةٌ` that pays off Unit 1's arc:

| | Before | After repair |
|---|---:|---:|
| Running words | 73 | **97** |
| Recycled tokens | 25 | 43 |
| **Share** | 41.0% | **52.4%** |

**Still below floor.** Reaching 70% needs **roughly 49 further tokens of pure recycled material**, which would put Story 1 near **150 words** — and the majority of it would be Unit 1 revision, in the unit whose job is to teach location. **That is not a repair; it is a different story.**

### Why this is structural, not a drafting failure

**LC-2's 70–85% band assumes a large established base.** It was written for a line where a unit adds three to five items to a corpus built over many units. **Unit 2's entire established base is one unit old — 28 carded entries.** A unit that introduces five new core items and four new support nouns, and whose communicative content *is* that new language, cannot reach 70% recycled tokens in a Novice-length text.

**And Unit 1 was exempt from this measurement** (LC-6: *"Unit 1 is exempt from the share measurement — there is no prior language"*). **Unit 2 is the first unit ever to face LC-2 in this course, and it fails on the first attempt.**

## 5 · Consolidated exception report — one item, escalated

**The Editor cannot resolve this.** SC-078 lists *"violate or amend a hard band"* as an escalation trigger.

> **Unit 2's two readings measure 41.0% and 46.3% recycled against LC-2's 70–85% band. One repair pass reached 52.4% and stalled. The shortfall is structural: the band presumes an established base that a second unit does not have.**

**Three options, with what each costs.**

**(a) Extend Unit 1's exemption to the early units, by ruling.** LC-6 already exempts Unit 1 *"because there is no prior language."* The same reasoning applies with diminishing force to Units 2 and 3. A ruling could set the band to apply **from Unit 4**, or scale it — say ≥50% at Unit 2, ≥60% at Unit 3, 70–85% from Unit 4. **Cost:** an amendment to a hard band, on the record. **This is the option the evidence supports**, and the shape of the ramp is the owner's to set.

**(b) Accept a documented per-unit exception for Unit 2.** Manual §3.2 item 6 permits *"an explicitly documented, approved exception recorded in the unit QC."* **Cost:** Unit 3 will need one too, and probably Unit 4, at which point the band is being suspended rather than applied.

**(c) Rewrite both readings to ~150 words each, mostly Unit 1 material.** **Cost:** the readings stop being about location. **Not recommended**, and it is the failure mode SC-051 names — a text inflated to hit a number.

**What is not in doubt:** both stories are otherwise sound. Every form is licensed, every floor is met, both object-state tables hold, the paradigm is contained, and the four rule-22 differences are realized on the page. **Only the band blocks them.**
