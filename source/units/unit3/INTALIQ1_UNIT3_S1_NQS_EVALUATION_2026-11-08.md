## Smallest revisions that address the actual weaknesses (NQS Stage 6)

1. **Make Layla's demand a fair trade a child would make**, with the unit's own target verb: «أُعْطِيكَ الْكُرَةَ، وَأَنْتَ تُعْطِينِي كِتَابَكَ.» The repetition is then plot-caused. (Form check owed: `تُعْطِينِي` with the object suffix.)
2. **Let the pictures give the parents their reasons**, at no word cost: frame S1-4 shows the mother at a table with a paper and no pen; S1-5 shows the father dressed to go out, at the door, with no key. (Art plan rows to amend.)
3. **Keep the draft's ending, cut its loop**: cat looks at the ball → «هَلْ تُرِيدُ الْكُرَةَ أَيْضًا؟» → he gives it → they play → Layla's question → «الْكُرَةُ عِنْدَ الْقِطِّ!». Drop lines 89–95 and «وَيَضْحَكُ الْجَمِيعُ».
4. Give the returning ball an agent if the play stays (the cat pushes it back).
