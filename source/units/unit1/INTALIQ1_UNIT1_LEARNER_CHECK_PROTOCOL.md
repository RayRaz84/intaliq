# Intaliq 1 — Unit 1 learner / panel check: protocol and instrument (not yet run)

*Editor (Text role) · 2026-10-11 · SC-121. The last condition for calling Unit 1 complete is a small instructional verification with people, which no build can do. This is the instrument for it — the questions, the materials, the sheet, and what counts — so it can be run by the owner, a panel educator or a teacher in one sitting of about 40 minutes with three to six children (ages 8–13, decoding assumed, no Arabic vocabulary assumed), or by the expert panel in the owner's place (charter §8 as amended by SC-009). **It has not been run; nothing below is a result.***

## 1 · What it tests — three questions only

| # | Question | What is observed | What would count as a pass for this unit |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | **Cold comprehension.** Can a learner who has done only Phase 1 (the picture spread and the digital vocabulary pathway) understand Reading 1 on a first, unassisted read? | after reading «قَائِمَةُ رَزَان» alone, the learner answers the four صَحٌّ أَمْ خَطَأٌ؟ items and the five مَنْ يَقُولُ؟ items on the page, without help; the observer notes which strip frame the learner points to | ≥ 3 of 4 true/false and ≥ 3 of 5 who-says right for most of the group, from the page alone; the observer records where the reading stalled (which paragraph, which word) |
| B | **Survey-to-writing transfer.** Does the survey (C1, Phase 3) produce what the parallel story (N2, Phase 4) needs? | the group runs C1 from notebook p. 9 with the three printed questions; each learner then completes p. 5–6 **alone** from their own grid | every learner has ≥ 3 names on the grid; p. 5–6 completed with real names from the grid, the صَدِيقِي / صَدِيقَتِي line chosen correctly for the friend named; the observer notes any question the learner could not ask without the teacher's voice |
| C | **Independent notebook completion.** Can a learner finish p. 7 («قِصَّتِي») with only the page in front of them? | p. 7 completed alone: the four frames, the two banks, the printed models, the speech bank | all four lines completed with bank words in the right slots (a thing after عِنْدَهُ / عِنْدَهَا, a person after «لِي ___»), a drawing, no question asked of an adult; the observer records what was asked |

Nothing else is measured. No scores are kept beyond this sheet; no child's name leaves the room (SC-048 item 7 applies to the pilot as to the platform).

## 2 · Materials (all already produced; print from Version 46)

Student book Unit 1 (`#unit-01/student`, learner view), notebook Unit 1 (`#unit-01/notebook`), one shared device on the vocabulary pathway (`#unit-01/practice` → الْمُفْرَدَات) for Phase 1, the teacher's page for the C1 procedure, and this sheet. No audio (none is verified) — the check is explicitly of the **cold-read** design the Activity Architecture §8 names as the oral gap.

## 3 · Running order (one sitting)

1. Phase 1 — 10 min: the picture spread, then the digital vocabulary pathway on the shared device (48 tasks; stop at 10 min wherever the group is and note the task number).
2. Reading 1, alone — 8 min: each learner reads «قَائِمَةُ رَزَان» silently, then does صَحٌّ أَمْ خَطَأٌ؟ and مَنْ يَقُولُ؟ on the page. Observer records answers and stall points (**A**).
3. C1 — 7 min: notebook p. 9, the printed questions read aloud once by the teacher, then learners ask each other. Observer notes the questions learners could not ask alone (**B**).
4. N2 — 8 min: p. 5–6 alone from the grid (**B**).
5. N3 — 7 min: p. 7 alone (**C**).
6. The observer fills §4 before anyone leaves.

## 5 · What the result changes

A pass on A, B and C, recorded in the register with the sheet attached, closes the "final instructional verification" condition for Unit 1. A fail on A sends the preparation sequence (the vocabulary pathway's coverage, the strip) back for redesign, not the stories; a fail on B sends the survey questions or the N2 frame back; a fail on C sends the N3 models and banks back. In every case the frozen stories stand (SC-093).
