## 1. What the existing check can and cannot establish

The existing instrument checks cold Reading 1 comprehension, survey-to-N2 transfer and independent N3 completion. These are useful questions. They do not establish delayed retention, listening comprehension, speaking performance across the unit, Reading 2 comprehension or use of the authentic corner.

Its opening gives ten minutes for a 48-task preparation pathway and explicitly permits stopping before completion. A learner who reaches only part of that pathway has received partial preparation. Record that condition; do not classify their difficulty as failure after the promised preparation.

The running order moves from Reading 1 directly to C1, although C1 draws on possession language also reinforced in Reading 2. Its third-person/friendship choices also depend on the printed apparatus. A diagnostic sitting may intentionally restrict input, but it must not be called a trial of the complete five-phase unit. Teacher reading the C1 questions once is planned rehearsal; any further prompting is separately recorded.

The protocol permits an expert panel in place of children under SC-009; SC-121 says the learner check must be run to close Unit 1. Reconcile the closure wording explicitly. Panel inspection is valuable design evidence and must remain labeled as such.

## 2. Run two distinguishable checks

### Check A: the cold-reading diagnostic

Retain the existing three-question instrument as a diagnostic, with its scope stated. Verify decoding and describe prior Arabic exposure. Record exactly how much Phase 1 was completed and which language was established. If preparation is unfinished, either complete it before the reading or report a restricted-preparation result. Record text access, picture support and all adult interventions.

Retain the four true/false and five speaker items as page checks. Add a brief meaning confirmation through pointing to textual support or identifying who wants a friend and what the pen does. Use already authorized language and low-output responses. Do not interpret picture-only success as independent text comprehension.

The existing 3/4 and 3/5 benchmarks remain local descriptive indicators pending review. Report the actual responses and support conditions; a majority meeting those counts does not close the other functions.

### Check B: performance after the actual unit sequence

Run within ordinary teaching; do not compress the full unit into the forty-minute diagnostic. Let observed learner processing determine pacing and record the actual exposure.

| Stage | Observe | Resource/support |
|---|---|---|
| Input and repeated processing | Can learners identify meaning across more than one occurrence? Where does decoding or meaning break down? | Actual phrase pages and digital pathways; record completion and use of preview speech |
| Both readings | Can learners follow who is speaking, the friendship problem and the pen's unexpected intervention? | Canon and strips; separate textual evidence from picture inference |
| C1 → N2 | Do exchanged names become appropriate names in the parallel writing? Does the learner understand the answers they mark? | Printed questions, models, grid and N2; planned rehearsal allowed |
| N1 / N3 | Are framed lines meaningful and referents coherent? Does the learner select things/people appropriately? | Banks and models; past forms copied, not formed; no new paradigm demand |
| C3 / C4 | Can the learner make a short supported contribution and respond appropriately to a partner? | Completed page, familiar language and teacher frame cue appropriate to Captions |
| Functional texts | Can the learner locate a named person or useful information? | Roster; authentic-photo tasks only when supplied |

Do not award independent production for a copied past form. Do not impose a free retell or a fully plotted original story on the Unit 1 Captions rung. Do not assess support-only forms productively.

## 3. Listening and authentic materials

Until the recordings are verified and bound, record listening as **not delivered / not observed**. Preview synthesis does not satisfy SC-028. Once clips exist, choose at least one phrase-to-picture and one sentence/speaker comprehension probe. Present sound before transcript, allow the planned replay, record it, and record extra prompting. Inspect pronunciation/clip accuracy separately from learner comprehension.

Until the real photographs exist, record authentic-corner performance as **not observed**. Once supplied, verify that the intended name/letter recognition is possible from the actual photograph at the delivered size. Do not count a photograph's presence as evidence that its task works.

## 4. Later check and small transfer

At the next available lesson, preferably 3–7 days later for this local trial, use a brief check before review. Record the actual interval and intervening exposure. This timing is proposed guidance, not a new pacing requirement.

- Revisit one identity/friendship meaning and one possession meaning through familiar, authorized language with a different response order or licensed recombination.
- Run a short exchange with another partner, still using the printed question/model support if the rung permits it.
- Ask for a changed name/object caption or framed line drawn from the licensed bank. Keep the change small enough that new language is not the explanation for failure.
- Once recordings exist, include a listening meaning check using a verified clip and record picture/replay support.

A repeated verbatim story response supports memory of that response. Changed-context success supports bounded transfer. Neither establishes domain-general proficiency. Write the probes and confirm their language permissions before administering them; do not improvise new test language on the day.

## 5. What triggers revision

Missing/ambiguous resources, incoherent referents or language outside the envelope trigger a materials correction. Repeated difficulty after actual preparation triggers review of input load, repeated processing or supports. Successful grid completion without understanding prompts a meaning check. Failure after incomplete preparation is evidence about pacing or delivery, not a clean test of learning.

Use the course evidence template to record: function → observation → explanation to test → source correction → expected improvement → recheck. Preserve the frozen stories unless their own meaning or discourse is the identified problem and the owner reopens them.
