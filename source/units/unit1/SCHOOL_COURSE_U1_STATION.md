# Unit 1 Input Station — «قَبْلَ الْقِرَاءَة» (spec v1)

*The School Course · the online supplement's in-class input station, per SC-023 · runs at the start of class on shared devices, before the unit's reading · 10–12 minutes, pairs or small groups · input-only: the learner clicks, drags, and listens — never types, never produces · fully independent per SC-024: no teacher mediation, no props; the no-device path is the textbook's input pages (B1/B2)*

## Design rules (platform-wide, set here by precedent)

1. **Input only.** The station makes meaning before the book asks for reading. No typing, no speaking into the device, no scores displayed against other learners — a completion mark only.
2. **Same envelope, same sweep.** Station text and audio obey the unit's ledger exactly; the numerals rule (SC-022) and all style law apply. Station encounters are counted in the unit's LC-6 arithmetic.
3. **Audio-first.** Every item speaks; text appears with or after sound, never before it. The station is where the course's audio strand begins.
4. **Ritual formats.** Like the print catalog, station activities are a small closed set of recurring interaction types with fixed icons — a new type requires a register ruling.
5. **Fallback is print, not props (SC-024).** Where devices are absent, the input phase runs on the textbook's own input pages (B1 picture spread, B2 preview panel) — fully independent, nothing made, nothing acted. No teacher-led or prop-based fallbacks exist.

## The six activities (U1 instance ≈ the platform's founding format set)

**A1 · Faces and names (tap).** The four cast portraits; a voice says «هٰذِهِ هُدَى… هٰذَا عُمَر…»; the learner taps the matching face. Then reversed: the face highlights, three names appear, tap the right one. *Pre-teaches: cast, هذا/هذه, names as sight words. (Discharges the pointing pair's floor — the v2 story narrates rather than points.)*

**A2 · Word garden (tap).** Illustrated core nouns — وَلَد، بِنْت، قَائِمَة، صَدِيق، صَدِيقَة — spoken, then tapped from a scene. Three rounds, shuffled.

**A3 · Verbs in motion (tap the clip).** Three short animations loop side by side: a child *entering* a room, a child *seeing* something (look + point-of-view shot), a child *saying* (speech bubble fills). Voice: «تَدْخُلُ… تَرَى… تَقُولُ» and masculine counterparts. Learner taps the clip that matches the word. *This is where the story's verbs are born.*

**A4 · Who has it? (drag).** Characters and objects: a voice says «عِنْدَهُ قَائِمَةٌ» / «لَيْسَ عِنْدَهَا قَلَمٌ» — the learner drags the object to (or away from) the character. The ليس items show the object greyed and crossed as it's dragged away. *Pre-teaches the possession chunks and their negation as meaning, long before any grammar notice.*

**A5 · Story preview (watch and tap).** Scene 1 of «قَائِمَةُ لِينَا» animated with full audio — the doorway, the two narration lines, the greeting — stopping exactly where the reading will take over. One comprehension tap: «عِنْدَهَا قَائِمَةٌ؟» (tap نَعَمْ / لَا). *The reading begins in class with the learner already inside the story.*

**A6 · Likes (tap) · يُحِبُّ ⭐.** Per SC-027, يُحِبّ items connect a person to a THING or activity, never to a named person: voice «تُحِبُّ لِينَا الْقَائِمَةَ» / «يُحِبُّ عُمَر الْقَلَمَ»; the learner taps the person and the thing, and a small star links them. The item pool grows as later units license more objects. *Pre-teaches يُحِبّ as relation, not vocabulary card — and never as romance.*

## Flow and count

Fixed order A1→A6 (meaning → words → verbs → possession → story → likes), ~2 minutes each, completion mark at the end. Station encounter contributions (counted in the unit ledger): each core item ≥4 audio exposures across the set; يَدْخُل/يَرَى/يَقُول/يُحِبّ/لَيْسَ عِنْدَ reach their with-activities floors here by design.

## Open items

Platform choice (owner's — the spec is platform-agnostic: any tool that does tap/drag/audio serves) · voice talent (one warm female narrator + one child voice minimum; audio governance to be written when the audio strand formalizes) · whether station completion data is visible to the teacher (recommend: completion only, no analytics on children — flag for an owner privacy ruling before any platform is configured).
