*For the voice — a native MSA speaker, or since **SC-129** a speech synthesiser run against these exact texts — and for the verifying listener, who is a named person in every case. ## 1 · What to record

72 short clips: every sentence of the two Unit 1 stories, in order, and one phrase for each core word. **Story 1** «قَائِمَةُ رَزَان» — clips 1–46 (24 sentences, then 22 phrases). **Story 2** «مَنْ أَنْتَ يَا قَلَم؟» — clips 47–72 (11 sentences, then 15 phrases). Recording the sentences in order, as a story, gives the most natural reading; then cut one file per row.

## 2 · How to read

- **One voice per reading** (SC-130): Reading 1's 46 clips in one voice, Reading 2's 26 in one voice — a native speaker of Modern Standard Arabic or one fixed synthesised voice (SC-129) — named per reading in the manifest (`voice`); never mix within a reading.
- **Exactly as printed, fully vocalised.** Pronounce every printed vowel, including the final case endings (no pausal dropping). **Names are printed without final vowels — read them that way** (رَزَان · عُمَر · سَارَة · هُدَى · طَارِق · رَنَا · كَرِيم · فَرِيدَة · مَهَا · زَيْد).
- **Child-directed pace**: clear and unhurried, not slow-motion. Narration as narration; words inside «…» as speech, with natural feeling (surprise, protest, greeting).
- «…» inside a line marks a short dramatic pause — make it audible.
- No words added, dropped, repeated or paraphrased. A clip that differs from its text in any word or ending is re-recorded, never edited.

## 3 · File format

Mono · 44.1 kHz · **MP3 at 128 kbps or higher** (or OGG Vorbis) · one clip per file · silence before and after ≤ 0.3 s · peak-normalised, no clipping, no background noise or music · **filename exactly as in the table** (lower-case, the path below `assets/audio/`). Deliver one folder `audio/` containing `unit-01/` (46 files) and `unit-01p/` (26 files), plus the filled tracker (`INTALIQ1_UNIT1_CLIP_TRACKER.csv`).

## 5 · Verification — by a named Arabic-reading listener, clip by clip

For **each** file, the listener plays it while reading the text in the table and ticks all six; any "no" → **retake**, with the reason in the tracker.

1. The file name matches the row.
2. Every word is present, in order — nothing added, dropped or repeated.
3. Every vowel and every final ending matches the printed vocalisation (names without final vowels).
4. Speech is natural MSA at a child-directed pace; dialogue sounds like speech.
5. Clean audio: no noise, clipping, echo or cut-off; silence ≤ 0.3 s at each end.
6. Mono, MP3 ≥ 128 kbps (or OGG).

When all six hold, write **accepted**, the listener's full name and the date (YYYY-MM-DD) in the tracker. Nobody else may mark a clip accepted, and no clip counts as verified until a named listener has accepted it.

## 6 · After delivery

The Editor checks the tracker against the files, computes each file's SHA-256, binds the 72 accepted clips into the unit data, rebuilds, plays clips in the built pages, and walks every Unit 1 pathway (the listening tasks switch on only when all 72 bindings are valid). Retakes come back in the same names.
